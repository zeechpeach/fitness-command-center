PUTTING THIS ON YOUR SITE
=========================
Browser only. No terminal, no token, no VS Code. About 3 minutes.

STEP 1  Unzip this file (double-click it on a Mac).
        You should see: index.html, a folder called src, and this file.

STEP 2  In your browser go to:
        https://github.com/zeechpeach/fitness-command-center

STEP 3  Near the top right, click the "Add file" button.
        A small menu drops down. Click "Upload files".

STEP 4  You now see a big dashed box that says
        "Drag files here to add them to your repository".

        Open the unzipped folder next to your browser window.
        Drag these TWO items into that box:
             index.html
             the src folder
        Do NOT drag this READ-ME-FIRST file.

STEP 5  Wait a few seconds. Under the box you'll see it list
        3 files. That is correct: index.html, src/js/app.js,
        and src/css/styles.css.

STEP 6  Scroll down to the "Commit changes" box.
        In the first line type:
             Rebuild program creation, scheduling, and mobile fixes

STEP 7  Leave "Commit directly to the main branch" selected.
        Click the green "Commit changes" button.

STEP 8  Wait about 2 minutes while GitHub rebuilds your site.
        Then open your site and hold Shift while clicking reload.

DONE.


IF ANYTHING LOOKS WRONG
=======================
Nothing here is permanent.
   Go to the repo -> click "Commits" -> find your commit
   -> click the "Revert" button.
That puts the site back exactly as it was before.


THE FIRST THING TO DO AFTER UPLOADING
=====================================
Open Settings (the gear icon, top right) and build your program.
Creation actually works now, and whatever you create becomes your
active program automatically.


GETTING YOUR PROGRAM IN (do this first, after uploading)
=======================================================
  Settings now has an "Import a program" button.

  1. Open my-program.txt (in this folder). Select ALL of it and copy.
  2. In the app, tap the gear icon, then "Import a program".
  3. Paste into the box and tap "Check it".
  4. You should see "Chain Rebuild (Physique First), 7 days".
  5. Tap the green button to add it and make it active.

  That puts all 43 exercises in, across all seven days, with the right
  sets, reps and tracking types already set. No typing.


LOGGING YOUR DAY IN PARTS
========================
  MORNING PRIMER AND PRE-BED ROUTINE now have a "Mark done" button in
  their header. One tap. It shows your streak and how many of the last
  30 days you have hit. These are stored separately from workouts, so
  ticking the primer never touches your session or your training
  numbers.

  YOUR SESSION CAN BE FINISHED LATER. Log what you did at lunch, tap
  Complete Session, then come back in the evening. The app reloads what
  you already saved, tells you so, and saving again UPDATES that same
  session instead of creating a second one for the same day.

  Your sets also save continuously as you type, so leaving the app
  mid-session loses nothing.


A TAP PROBLEM YOU MAY HAVE NOTICED
==================================
  On any phone screen, six invisible full-screen panels were sitting on
  top of the whole app and swallowing your taps. They were left over
  from the food pop-ups. This is very likely part of why the app felt
  unresponsive even after it had finished loading. Fixed.


TWO NEW THINGS IN SETTINGS AND THE CALENDAR
===========================================
  EXPORT YOUR DATA. Settings now has an "Export all data" button that
  downloads every workout, program, weigh-in and food log as one file
  you keep. Do this every few months. Until now there was no copy of
  your training history anywhere except Google's servers.

  DELETE A BAD SESSION. Tap any day on the Calendar and there is now a
  "Delete this session" button. Before this, a workout logged on the
  wrong date was permanent and quietly corrupted your PRs and your
  discipline score forever.


HOW LOGGING WORKS NOW
=====================
  Each exercise is tracked the way it should be, and the app guesses
  correctly from the name when you add it:

    Weight and reps   barbell, dumbbell, machine, cable work
    Reps only         push-ups, prone swimmers, scapular pull-ups
    Time in seconds   holds, planks, balance, levers, hangs
    Duration in mins  treadmill walks, bike, any steady cardio
    Just a checkbox   stretches, mobility, the morning primer

  So a hollow body hold gives you three "sec" boxes, not a weight box.
  Going 30 to 45 seconds now registers as progress, and the Copy button
  says "last: 30 sec" instead of a meaningless weight.

  You can override the guess. In Settings, when you add or edit an
  exercise, there is a "How do you track it?" dropdown.


WHAT CHANGED THIS TIME
======================

WORKOUT CREATION (the thing you said was broken)
  Three separate bugs made it look broken, and all three hit on your
  very first attempt:
   - Adding an exercise closed the panel you were working in, so the
     exercise you just added disappeared from view.
   - A day with no exercises showed a completely blank white screen
     with no message.
   - Nothing activated the program you built, so the logger kept
     showing the default plan the app makes on its own.
  Also fixed: naming a day no longer uses those little browser popup
  boxes (which stop working entirely if you ever tap "prevent this
  page from creating more dialogs"), renaming a day now keeps its
  exercises instead of emptying it, an apostrophe in a name no longer
  breaks every button, and tapping Back while it saves no longer
  creates duplicate copies of your program or shows a false error.

YOUR SESSION IS NO LONGER FRAGILE
  Before: reloading, taking a phone call, or tapping a day button
  mid-workout erased everything you had entered. Now your session is
  saved continuously and comes back automatically. Switching days
  asks first.

FINISHING A WORKOUT WITH NO SIGNAL
  Before: in a basement gym, "Complete Session" did nothing at all,
  forever, with no message. Tapping repeatedly queued up duplicate
  copies that appeared later. Now it saves to your phone first, tells
  you it saved, and syncs when you get signal.

TYPING ON A PHONE
  The weight and reps boxes made iOS zoom the whole page every single
  time you tapped one, and the buttons around them were too small.
  Fixed, plus you now get the big number keypad instead of the
  cramped punctuation one.

FEWER POPUPS
  Around 80 popup dialogs replaced with a small message that slides
  up and disappears. Popups on every save had trained you to tap OK
  without reading, which is exactly what made the important warnings
  useless.
